Company PAC Donation Superset

MAIN FILE: company_pac_superset.csv

Purpose

This dataset identifies whether U.S. companies control Political Action Committees (PACs) and whether those PACs have made any political donations, based on Federal Election Commission (FEC) data and Goods Unite Us (GUU) Snowflake models.

It enables a definitive classification of companies into:

Companies with PACs that have donated

Companies with PACs that have not donated

Companies with no PACs (implicit)

Data Sources

FEC PAC master & summary files (official PAC registrations and receipts)

Snowflake: ANALYTICS.MRT_MARKETPLACE.COMPANY_PAC_CONTRIBUTIONS

Snowflake: ANALYTICS.WAREHOUSE.DIM_COMPANY (company universe)

Key Columns

TICKER – Public company ticker

has_pac – Whether the company controls at least one PAC

has_pac_donations – Whether any controlled PAC has donated

pac_count – Number of PACs controlled by the company

active_pac_count – Number of PACs with donation activity

total_pac_amount – Total PAC donations across all cycles

pac_status – One of:

PAC_DONATED

PAC_NO_DONATIONS

NO_PAC

Interpretation

If a company appears in this file:

Its PAC donation status is fully accounted for.
If a company does not appear as donating:

It has either never donated via a PAC or does not control a PAC.

ADITIONAL FILES:
fec_pacs_no_donations: Corporate PACs that exist but show zero donation activity.
fec_pacs_with_donation_flag: Corporate PACs annotated with a has_donated flag based on observed contribution activity.
company_pac_donation_status: PAC activity rolled up to the connected organization level, including: PAC count, Active PAC count, Total PAC donation amount, PAC donation flags
