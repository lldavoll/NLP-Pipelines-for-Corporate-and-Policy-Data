1. File Description:

The file for this Task is called company_contacts_full.csv and contains the final results of the contact-information extraction  made by web scrapping.
Each row corresponds to one company, with fields for official domain, Investor Relations (IR) information, Customer Service (CS) information, and social media profiles.

The dataset has:

4631 total companies

9 columns

2. Column	Description
company_clean:	Normalized company name used for search and matching
domain:	Identified official corporate domain
ir_page: URL of the company’s Investor Relations page
ir_email: Extracted Investor Relations email address
cs_page: Customer service or general contact page
cs_email: Extracted customer service/support email
twitter_url: Official Twitter/X account (if found)
facebook_url: Official Facebook page (if found)
bluesky_url: Official Bluesky profile (if found)

3. Some considerations:

All company_clean values are present.
Most missing fields reflect companies that do not publish emails or use webforms only.
Social media fields are frequently empty because: Not all companies have active accounts.
Many accounts are not official or not easily discoverable.
Bluesky is almost entirely empty (normal).
Overall the accuracy is really high and reliable
