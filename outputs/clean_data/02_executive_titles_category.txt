Dataset Overview

Files: executive_titles_category.csv // executives_final.csv
Contents: I group all the titles in 12 standardized executive titles grouped by category and their total occurrences across filings.

Column	Description
title_standard:	Normalized executive title (e.g., Chief Executive Officer, Vice President)
category: Broader classification of each role (e.g., C-Suite: CEO, Director, VP/EVP/SVP)
count: Number of times the title appears across all filings

Things I found:
In the SEC filings documents the five most frequent titles are: CEO, Director, Vice President, CFO, and President.
The specialized Roles Are Less Frequent, positions like CTO, General Counsel, COO, and Treasurer appear in far fewer filings.
All other roles that are hard to classify I captured under Other Executive to preserve full record counts (Titles such as: Chairman, Officer, Assistant, etc)

The Visualization:
For this case I created 3 types of plots:

1. Executive Titles (Bar Chart): This plot shows the total frequency of each standardized executive title.
Chief Executive Officer and Director are the most common titles across filings.
Vice President, CFO, and President form the next most frequent tier.
Specialized roles (e.g., CTO, General Counsel, Treasurer) appear much less often, reflecting variation in company structures.

File: bar_chart.png


2. Executive Categories (Pie Chart): The pie chart aggregates titles into broader categories.
The C-Suite cluster (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CMO, Legal) together make up nearly half of all executives.
Directors and Presidents contribute about one-third.
Vice Presidents represent a key layer of mid-to-upper management (13%).
Smaller slices include Corporate Secretary and Finance Officers.

File: category_pie.png


3. Title Frequency Distribution (Histogram): This histogram illustrates how often titles occur across filings with a linear scale.
Most executive titles appear fewer than 500 times a long tail of rare or company-specific positions.
A few leadership titles (CEO, Director, VP, CFO, President) occur over 1,000 times, forming a clear high-frequency cluster.
Is clear that the data shows a bimodal distribution: a small set of ubiquitous titles and a large set of rare ones.
File: histogram_frequencies.png
