FILE: official_board_exec_changes.csv

Description: The dataset contains executive changes extracted from Official Board alert emails. The emails report updates to company leadership such as appointments, 
role changes, and departures.

FIELDS:
email_date: The date when the alert email was sent.

company: The name of the company where the executive change occurred.

industry: The industry classification of the company.

subsidiary_of: The parent company if the organization is listed as a subsidiary in the alert. 
If the company is not listed as a subsidiary, this field may be empty.

section_type: The category under which the event appears in the Official Board alert.
(Appointments,changes)

event_type: The specific type of executive movement described in the alert.
(joined, became, promoted, left, etc).

executive_name: The name of the executive.

old_position: The executive’s previous role.

new_position: The new or current role of the executive.

effective_date: The date when the executive change takes effect (if specified in the alert).

posted_date: The date when the executive change was posted.

raw_event_text: The original sentence describing the executive change as extracted from the email.
